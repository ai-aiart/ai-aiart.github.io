About

Bringing AI into the radiotherapy workflow

AI-ART started as a PhD research line at Politecnico di Milano, in close collaboration with the radiotherapy department of Humanitas Research Hospital. We study computer vision and artificial intelligence for adaptive radiation therapy (ART).

ART continuously adjusts a treatment plan to the patient's anatomy as it changes during the course of therapy. Instead of irradiating large regions, it concentrates dose on the target and spares healthy tissue. This matters most when tumours lie close to critical organs.

Each adaptation cycle involves imaging, contouring of targets and organs at risk, and re-planning by a multidisciplinary team. Our mission is to build and openly share methods that make these steps faster and more reproducible, and safer to automate.

Research themes

01

Auto-segmentation

Deep learning contouring of targets and organs at risk, including the planning target volume and lymph nodes for total marrow and lymph node irradiation (TMLI).

02

Image synthesis & inpainting

CBCT-to-CT translation, synthetic CT generation and 3D inpainting with GANs and diffusion models.

03

Trustworthy AI

Uncertainty estimation for segmentation QA, efficient explainability for 3D models, and fairness analysis across patient groups.

04

Planning automation

Learning-based optimisation of VMAT field geometry, automatic base-dose planning, and ML-driven patient-specific quality assurance.
